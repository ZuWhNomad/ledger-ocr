================================================================================
LEDGER OCR - GETTING STARTED GUIDE
================================================================================

Ledger OCR turns bank statements and general ledgers into clean tables. It reads
PDFs, scans, photos, and Excel and Word files, and saves the result as CSV,
Excel (.xlsx), or Word (.docx) - entirely on your local computer. Your financial
data is never uploaded to the internet or cloud.


1. INSTALLATION
- Double-click LedgerOCR-Setup.exe and follow the on-screen prompts.
- Shortcuts will be created on your Desktop and in your Start menu.
- No administrator permissions are required; the app installs just for you.


2. RUNNING THE APP
- Open "Ledger OCR" from your Desktop or Start menu (or double-click run.cmd).
- Your default web browser will automatically open a local page.


3. PROCESSING A DOCUMENT
- Drag your PDF, image, Excel, or Word file directly onto the drop area, or
  click to choose a file from your computer.
- Wait a few seconds while the document is processed.
- A table preview of your transactions will appear on screen.
- Rows that the tool detects may contain a math error are highlighted for
  your review.


4. DOWNLOADING YOUR TABLE
- Click "Download CSV", "Download XLSX" (Excel), or "Download DOCX" (Word).
- The file will be saved directly to your Downloads folder.
- You can open and edit it immediately in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or
  Microsoft Word. Flagged rows are highlighted in the Excel and Word files.


5. SCANNED DOCUMENTS AND PHOTOS
- PDFs exported directly from your bank or accounting software work
  immediately without extra setup.
- Scanned documents or smartphone photos require the free OCR add-on. Click
  "Install OCR add-on" inside the app (one click; approve the Windows security
  prompt if it appears).


6. OPTIONAL ERROR-CHECKER
- An optional local assistant can double-check only the rows flagged by the
  math verification.
- The assistant runs 100% on your machine and never modifies your numbers.
- It remains completely off unless installed and turned on.
- Choosing the assistant: the app checks which local assistants are installed
  and suggests the best one for your computer. To use a different one, click
  "Choose model" and pick from the list - your choice is remembered.
- (Advanced, safe to ignore: power users can also set the model with the
  OCR_LLM_MODEL setting.)


7. TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS
- Browser did not open: Double-click run.cmd or open "Ledger OCR" from Start.
- Scans not reading: Click "Install OCR add-on" in the app and accept prompts.
- Cannot find files: Look for the downloaded CSV, Excel, or Word file in your
  Downloads folder.
- Highlighted rows: Check flagged rows in the preview for potential math errors.
